Principal Software Engineer: Responsibilities [WIP]



1. Technical Strategy & Architecture

- Strategic Technical Planning: Independently translate complex functional and non-functional requirements into robust, executable technical roadmaps and implementation plan.

- System Design & Scalability: Drive implementation-level design decisions, ensuring systems are built for scalability and performance within established architectural guidelines.

- Technical Decision-Making: Act as the primary technical decision-maker for assigned areas, making informed trade-offs between delivery speed, quality, maintainability, and risk.

- Technical Debt Management: Identify, communicate, and help prioritize technical debt within assigned areas based on business impact, delivery risk, maintainability, and long-term system health.

- Risk Management: Proactively identify and mitigate critical technical risks to ensure the delivery of stable, secure, and reliable systems.



2. Execution & Ownership

- Implementation Ownership: Own and contribute hands-on to technically complex features, modules, integrations, migrations, and system improvements from design through implementation, release support, and stabilization.

- Estimation & Feasibility: Provide reliable technical estimates and feasibility assessments for complex features, modules, integrations, migrations, and uncertain technical areas by identifying assumptions, dependencies, risks, and unknowns.

- Advanced Problem Solving: Lead investigation and resolution of complex bugs, production issues, performance problems, integration failures, and system behavior problems.

- Production Readiness: Ensure critical solutions are ready for production by considering logging, monitoring, observability, failure handling, rollback strategy, data integrity, deployment impact, and supportability.



3. Innovation & Technical Growth

- Rapid Domain Mastery: Quickly adapt to and master new technologies, tech stacks, or complex legacy codebases and domains as project needs evolve.

- Practical R&D: Proactively explore and R&D technologies and tools beyond direct project exposure, expanding technical depth and suggesting appropriate tech to enhance team capability.



4. Collaboration

- Stakeholder Collaboration: Collaborate independently with clients, project leads, engineers, QA, DevOps, UI/UX, and other stakeholders to clarify requirements and resolve technical ambiguity.

- Technical Communication: Communicate complex technical topics clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

- Mentorship: Provide technical guidance and mentorship to engineers on complex design, implementation, debugging, and problem-solving areas when needed.
